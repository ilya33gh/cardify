Cardify is a modern, private, and 100% offline loyalty and discount card wallet for Android.

Digitize and organize all your loyalty cards, store passes, and discount memberships in one place with zero ads, zero analytics, and zero internet permission.

Features:
• 100% Offline & Private: No internet permission, no accounts, and no data tracking.
• Fullscreen POS Barcode Scanner View: Automatically maximizes screen brightness for fast checkout scanning.
• Fast Camera Scanner: Easily scan barcodes and QR codes from cards or gallery images.
• Dynamic Material You Theming: Expressive Material 3 design with light, dark, and OLED modes.
• Flexible Organization: Categorize cards with custom colors, notes, favorites, and search.
• Biometric Security & App Lock: Protect your cards with fingerprint, face unlock, or PIN code with customizable auto-lock timeout.
• Local Backup & Restore: Export and import your encrypted card database via JSON.
